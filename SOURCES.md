# Source Register

Checked: 2026-10-30. References appear near the claims they support in the articles.

Research sources were checked through their linked abstracts; no experiments were reproduced. Product descriptions are based on public documentation, not hands-on console testing. Proposed metrics and workflows are editorial methods, not vendor implementation specifications.
