```text
Scheduled answers = questions × surfaces × repetitions × windows
```

Select a mode: exploratory checks / periodic baseline / ongoing monitoring.

Document the trigger for review, minimum usable sample, comparison window, and person responsible. Ordinary fluctuation and consequential factual errors may need different escalation rules.

Before automating, manually review examples of a valid recommendation, an inappropriate recommendation, a refusal, a failed collection, and an ambiguous alias.

After one operating period, compare total review effort and useful decisions with the original estimate. Change cadence if the team is collecting observations faster than it can investigate them.
